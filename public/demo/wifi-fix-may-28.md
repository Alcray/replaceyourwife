# Fixed home Wi-Fi on May 28

Problem:
The Wi-Fi was connected but internet was not working.

Steps that worked:
1. Restarted modem.
2. Waited 60 seconds.
3. Restarted router.
4. Checked that WAN light was blinking.
5. Changed DNS to 8.8.8.8 and 1.1.1.1.
6. Internet came back after 3 minutes.

Router location:
Living room cabinet, bottom shelf.

Important:
Do not press the factory reset pin unless everything else fails.
