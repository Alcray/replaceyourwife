# Aroma AWK-151B Electric Kettle Home Memory

Source: Aroma AWK-151B electric kettle manual.

## Basic Operation

To boil water with the Aroma AWK-151B electric kettle, place the power base on a flat, dry, heat-resistant surface. Fill the kettle with water between the minimum and maximum fill lines. Close the lid securely, set the kettle on the base, plug the base into an outlet, and press the power switch down. The indicator light turns on while heating. The kettle shuts off automatically when the water reaches a boil.

## If The Kettle Does Not Heat

The Aroma AWK-151B has dry-boil protection. If it is turned on with too little water or no water, it can automatically shut off. To reset it, unplug the kettle, remove it from the base, let it cool for about 15 minutes, then refill it with water above the minimum line before trying again.

## Cleaning

Before cleaning the Aroma AWK-151B, unplug it and let it cool. Wipe the exterior with a damp cloth. Do not immerse the kettle, power base, cord, or plug in water. Do not use abrasive cleaners or scouring pads.

## Descaling

To remove mineral buildup, fill the kettle with a descaling solution or a mixture of white vinegar and water, let it sit, then boil and discard the liquid. Rinse thoroughly by boiling fresh water and discarding it until the vinegar smell is gone.

## Safety Warnings

Keep the lid closed while heating and pouring. Do not overfill past the maximum line because boiling water can spill. Use only the supplied power base. Keep the appliance away from children and hot surfaces.
