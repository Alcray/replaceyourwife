# Aroma AWK-151B Reset Card

If the Aroma AWK-151B electric kettle does not heat because dry-boil protection shut it off, reset it this way:

1. Unplug the kettle.
2. Remove the kettle from the power base.
3. Let the kettle cool for about 15 minutes.
4. Refill the kettle with water above the minimum fill line.
5. Put the kettle back on the power base and try heating again.

Safety: do not run the kettle empty, do not immerse the kettle or power base in water, and keep the base dry.
